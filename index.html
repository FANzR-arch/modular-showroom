Made slowly.

Every candle cures for two weeks before it leaves the studio. Every wick is trimmed and test-burned by hand.

Four ingredients.

Few materials, each chosen for a clean, even burn.

Wax

A blend of coconut and rapeseed wax. Soft and slow-burning, it holds scent well and melts to an even pool.

Fragrance oils

Each scent is blended in our studio from fragrance and essential oils, then left to settle before it meets the wax.

Cotton wick

A braided cotton wick, sized for each vessel so the flame stays steady and the pool reaches the edge.

Vessels

Clear glass, smoked glass or matte ceramic, from specialist makers. Every one is made to take a refill.
