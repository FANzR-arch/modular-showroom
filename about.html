About Solenne

Scent, made slowly.

Solenne is a small studio. We blend every scent ourselves and pour every candle and diffuser by hand, in small batches.

Twelve scents, two ways to live with them: candles and reed diffusers. The glass and ceramic vessels come from specialist makers, and each one is made to be refilled, not thrown away. They scent homes, hotels, stores and spas.
